# What is 'Cybersecurity'?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting not just the internet, but also computers, mobile devices, networks, local systems, clouds and much more from attacks, damages, unauthorized access, etc.
Just like how in the real world, the law enforcement maintains society by protecting citizens against crime, the digital world also requires similar people to maintain integrity and online safety.
Cybersecurity - this field is very important in the perspective of any netizen. Being able to protect yourself and others from malicious intentions of criminals is a very noteworthy skill to have.

# What are the types of Security?

Very broadly, there are two sides in this field- Offensive security and Defensive security. As an alias both sides are also known as the 'Red' team and the 'Blue' team.

# What is Offensive Security?

The Red team of this field is the Offensive security experts. Their job is to simulate an attack against systems in a controlled environment.
They help show the vulnerabilities on a system so that the Defenders can help patch, identify and prevent the same happening via hackers and malicious invaders.
This helps organizations to identify and fix security weaknessess so that they cannot be exploited by malicious attackers.

# What is Defensive Security?

The Blue team of this field is the Defensive security experts - The defenders. Their job is not just to stop and minimise the effect of breaches, but also to continuously monitor networks, analyse logs for suspicious activity, maintain system integrity, recover potentially lost data and more.
They have to make sure to identify and protect resources that are targeted by cyber criminals. After an attack, it is also their job to retrace the access points used by the malicious user and take appropriate actions.

# A Glance on the CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a framework in network and information security which lays down guidelines on how to efficiently protect data.
It stands for the following:
--  C - Confidentiality
--  I - Integrity
--  A - Availability

Confidentiality limits the access of data to unauthorized users. It ensures that data is accessible only to authenticated users in a system.
Integrity ensures that any data remains accurate and untouched by corruption and unauthorized modifications during storage or transmission.
Availability refers to the fact that given that a user is authorised to access said data, they must be able to do so at any point.
